Honk Out: Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Honk Out (on Google Play: "Car Escape: Honk Out Puzzle") is a traffic puzzle game for Android made by U Games ("we"). This page explains what information the game uses and why. In short: we do not collect your personal information. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show those ads.

What the game keeps on your phone

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, skins), your settings and which tips you have seen are saved only on your phone, inside the app. There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location, and this game data is never sent to us.

Uninstalling the game deletes this data. If you use Android's own backup, your phone may include it in your backup to your Google account, under Google's terms.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Video ads play only when you choose a button marked "AD". Short ad breaks can appear between levels. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location;
  • the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • how you interact with the app and ads (for example app launches, taps and video views);
  • diagnostic information, such as app performance.

This data goes to Google, not to us, and it is encrypted in transit. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: before any ad loads, Google's consent form asks for your choice. You can change it any time in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Everyone: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most phones). Without it, ads are not personalised.

Children

Honk Out is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for teenagers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Permissions

The game uses internet access (for ads) and vibration (for short haptic effects, which you can turn off in Settings). The Google Mobile Ads SDK adds the advertising ID and network state permissions it needs. The game does not ask for your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Changes

If the game starts using other services (for example a leaderboard or crash reports), we will update this page first and change the date above.
