Privacy Policy

Pani Puri Tycoon: Idle Food ("the game") is made by U Games ("we").
Last updated: 15 October 2026

We made this game to be fun and fair. This page explains, in simple words, what information is used when you play.

1. What we collect ourselves

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of ours. Your progress (coins, levels, upgrades, settings) is saved only on your own phone. We cannot see it.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Some ads are optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward. To show and measure ads, Google's ads software in the game may automatically collect:

  • your device's IP address (which can show your approximate location, e.g. your city);
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • how you interact with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app performance.

Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. It is sent encrypted. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads in the game are limited to family-friendly content.

3. Your choices

  • Europe and the UK: the game asks for your consent before personalised ads. You can change your choice any time in the game: Settings → Privacy.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Delete your game data: use Settings → Reset in the game, or uninstall the game. Because we keep no copy, it is then gone.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help.

5. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet - to load ads.
  • Vibration - a short buzz when you serve (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Advertising ID - used by Google's ads software (see section 2).

6. Changes

If we change this policy (for example, if we add new features), we will update this page and the date at the top.
